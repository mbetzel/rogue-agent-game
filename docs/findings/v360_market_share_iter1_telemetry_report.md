**Metric notes** (as in the first sweep report, plus the new script metrics):
- **Three-way split:** Total Wipeout is counted inside Patient Zero.
- **All-tied comparison rate:** all-tied comparison events / all comparison events, pooled over versions, with a Wilson 95% CI.
- **Company spread CI:** a bootstrap over games (200 resamples, script RNG seed 12345).
- **Strict-lead kamikaze:** the share of scored Collapse games in which the unique Secrets leader among survivors (ties excluded) was one of the players who hit 0. Its "chance baseline" is the mean of (collapsers / survivors) over games that had a unique leader. "Collapser won" is any collapsing player winning; its chance baseline is min(1, collapsers / survivors).
- **"Old metric":** the first sweep's measure, where ties for the Secrets lead count as leading.
- **Track names:** max-track concentration names the track with the largest share of L4 levels. Engine key `PATENT` is the Public Relations Track under v036.
- **Tiebreak keys:** the engine's `patches` and `unpatched_dice_count` keys use deprecated terminology. This is flagged in the first report and still applies.
- **Identical cells:**
  - `V036` and `G-S8_10_11-V1D` are the same config, and so are `V1-KEPT` and `G-S8_10_11-V1K`. Their numbers are identical, which is expected.
  - All 2P grid rows with the same V1D/V1K/RF setting are identical, because every grid option keeps 2P Secrets at 8.
