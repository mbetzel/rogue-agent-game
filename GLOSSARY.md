## 7. Glossary of Key Terms

> **Terminology Governance:** All changes to `RULES.md`, Print-and-Play assets (`pnp/`), and the Web UI (`web/`) must align strictly with this glossary. Any new term not defined below requires explicit confirmation before being introduced.

### 1. Core Identity & Roles

| Canonical Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Too Many Secrets** | Game Title | The primary title of the board game (codebase/simulation title: **Rogue Agent**). |
| **Project: SETEC (The Rogue Agent)** | Central Threat | The autonomous cracking program that serves as the central shared threat, compiling Attack and Corruption Cubes through the Cube Tower each round across three escalating versions (`v1.0`, `v2.0`, `v3.0`). |
| **White Hat Operative (White Hat Dossier)** | Player Identity & Asymmetric Setup | The specialist operative lead directed by each player (**The Operator**, **The Phreaker**, **The Acoustic**, **The Spook**, **The Prodigy**, **The Cipher**), contracted to defend a **Client Company's** infrastructure. Your **White Hat Dossier Card** defines the **Signature Protocol** triggered when rolling Face 6 (`Purple ★`). |
| **Client Company** | Asymmetric Setup | One of 6 enterprise clients (**Meridian Financial Trust**, **Omnidyne Systems**, **Aegis Defense Relay**, **Department of Telecommunications Oversight [DTO]**, **Helix Biomedical Consortium**, **The Prometheus Foundation**) contracted at setup. Grants a once-per-version **Institutional Override** (DTO *Slush Fund*: once per game) and names the color of the player's starting colored die (Red: Meridian, Prometheus; Blue: DTO, Aegis; Green: Omnidyne, Helix `[TUNE]`). Otherwise all Client Companies start with identical hardware and resources. |
| **Institutional Override** | Ability | A powerful corporate ability printed on a player's **Client Company Card** that may be activated **once per SETEC Version** (`v1.0`, `v2.0`, `v3.0`); DTO *Slush Fund* may be activated only **once per game**. Tracked on the **Institutional Override Track** as **Locked**, **Available**, **Used**, or **Forfeited**. |
| **Signature Protocol** | Ability | The bespoke operative ability printed on a player's **White Hat Dossier Card**, triggered whenever that player rolls Face 6 (`Purple ★`) on a **Purple Proprietary Specialist Die**. |
| **First Player (First Player Marker)** | Turn Order | The token designating the player who takes the first **Operations Turn** in **Phase 5: Operations**. Passes clockwise at the end of Phase 6 each round. |

### 2. Central Threat & Cube Tower Mechanics

| Canonical Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cube Tower (Mainframe Decryption Compiler)** | Component | The physical tower with internal baffles into which cubes from the **Staging Cup** are dropped during **Phase 2: Compiler Drop & Corruption**. Cubes either get stuck on the internal baffles or exit into the **Exit Tray**. |
| **Staging Cup (Relay Queue)** | Component / Zone | The cup holding **Compiler Cubes** and queued **Operative Signature Cubes** to be dropped into the **Cube Tower** during the next **Phase 2**. Its contents are public: any player may look into it at any time (cubes stuck inside the Cube Tower stay hidden). |
| **Exit Tray** | Component / Zone | The tray at the base of the **Cube Tower** where cubes land when they exit the tower during **Phase 2**. |
| **Internal Baffles (Stuck Cubes)** | Mechanic | Ledges inside the **Cube Tower** that trap dropped cubes, holding them in latent suspension until knocked loose in a future round. |
| **Pre-Seed Drop (Prime the Compiler)** | Setup Mechanic | The setup step where `1 Red, 1 Blue, 1 Green, and 1 Black Cube` are dropped into the empty **Cube Tower**; any cubes that exit into the tray are returned to the supply, leaving only stuck cubes inside to prime Round 1. |
| **Compiler Cubes** | Component | The four colors of cubes (**Red**, **Blue**, **Green**, **Black**) processed through the **Cube Tower**. Divided into **Black (Corruption) Cubes** and **Attack Cubes** (`Red`, `Blue`, `Green`). |
| **Attack Cubes** | Threat Category | Collectively refers to **Red**, **Blue**, and **Green** Compiler Cubes, which exit in **Phase 2**, resolve against players in **Phase 4: Attack Resolution**, and then recycle back into the **Staging Cup**. |
| **Operative Signature Cube (Signature Cube)** | Component / Threat | A custom-colored cube (1 per player in their player color: the 7th player cube) representing an operative’s network handshake signature in the Cube Tower. Starts on the Company Terminal Mat; moves to the **Staging Cup** when banking a Secret during **Phase 6 Step 4**; returns to the player's mat upon exiting in **Phase 2**. |
| **Target Lock** | Threat State | Temporary threat state triggered when an operative's **Signature Cube** exits the Cube Tower during **Phase 2**. In **Phase 4** (after Red, Blue and Green damage and the Patient Zero check), the locked player suffers the **Signature Attack** printed on the Central Board matching the most populous Compiler Cube color (`Green`, `Black`, `Blue`, or `Red`) in the **Exit Tray**, breaking ties in preset top-to-bottom priority order **`Green > Black > Blue > Red`** (fizzles if `0` cubes exited): **Green** places a green cube from the supply on the next space of `2` Research Tracks without green cubes (`2× 🔬` cost to advance; the **Pen Test Track** is eligible; each unplaceable cube gives `+2` **Vulnerability**); **Black** marks the player's **Institutional Override** `Used` for the current Version (or loses `2` **Corporate Secrets** if already used); **Blue** moves `1` **Gray Legacy Die** (from **Scanned Box**, else **In Production**) to the back of the **Processing Box** (`0` Secrets when triaged); **Red** lowers the locked player's **Market Share** by `2` and gives `+2` Market Share to the surviving rival with the least Market Share (locked rivals included; in 2-player games the **SETEC Corp** Black Cube counts; read after all locked players lose `2`). |
| **Red Cubes (Market Manipulation)** | Attack Cube | Attack cube resolved in **Phase 4**. Red cubes cannot be blocked: each exited Red cube lowers every surviving player's **Market Share** by `1` (not below `0`). Red cubes never cost Money or **Corporate Secrets**. *(Replaces Ransomware / Financial Wiretaps, retired in v0.36.0.)* |
| **Blue Cubes (Spyware / Data Exfiltration)** | Attack Cube | Attack cube resolved in **Phase 4**. Blue cubes cannot be blocked: each exited Blue cube steals **`1 Corporate Secret`** from every surviving player, in every version. |
| **Green Cubes (Malware / Logic Bombs)** | Attack Cube | Attack cube resolved in **Phase 4**, after Red and Blue, one cube at a time. Each Green cube hits the surviving player(s) with the highest **Vulnerability** at `4` or more (all tied players are hit): each loses Corporate Secrets by band (`4–6`: `1`, `7–9`: `2`, `10+`: `3`) `[TUNE]` and resets their Vulnerability to `0`. The next cube re-checks. A Green cube with no player at `4` or more has no effect; it still recycles. |
| **Black Cubes (Corrupted Code / Corruption)** | Threat Cube | Threat cube resolved in **Phase 2: Compiler Drop & Corruption**. Each exited Black cube advances the active **Version Card's Corruption Track** by 1 space and triggers that space's printed effect. Black cubes remain on the track and do not recycle. |
| **SETEC Version Card (`v1.0`, `v2.0`, `v3.0`)** | Component | One of three face-up roadmap cards defining SETEC's starting Staging Cup payload (`v1.0`) and each version's escalating **Corruption Track** (`4` spaces on `v1.0`, `5` spaces on `v2.0`, `6` spaces on `v3.0`). |
| **Corruption Track** | Board Track | The sequence of spaces on the active **SETEC Version Card** (`4 / 5 / 6` spaces). Each space covered by an exited **Black Cube** in Phase 2 immediately triggers its printed hazard (such as **Lose `$X`**, **Lose `X 🔬`**, **Lowest Vulnerability `+X`** (the player(s) with the lowest **Vulnerability** gain `X`), **Return `X` Colored Dice**, **Lose `X` Corporate Secrets**) or injects new cubes into the **Staging Cup**. |
| **Vulnerability Track** | Board Track | The `0–15` track on the Central SETEC Agent Board holding each player's Vulnerability marker. Spaces `0–3` are the safe zone (never hit by Green cubes); the bands `4–6`, `7–9` and `10–15` are printed with their Green damage (`−1 / −2 / −3` Corporate Secrets) `[TUNE]`. |
| **Vulnerability (⚠)** | Player State | How exposed a player's systems are to SETEC's Green malware, tracked by their Vulnerability marker on the **Vulnerability Track** (start `0`, max `15`; excess is lost; never below `0`). Rises by `1` per ⚠ icon on the final faces of a player's rolled dice at lock-in (**Phase 1 Step 4**; every **Secret face**, one high-output core face per colored die and three Gray faces carry ⚠, `★` faces never do; on a choice face it counts whichever option is chosen) and from some **Corruption Track** spaces and **Target Lock** fallbacks. Falls through the **Pen Test Track**, *B3 Air-Gapped Backup*, *G1 Self-Healing Kernel* and banking a Green **Secret face**; resets to `0` when hit by a Green cube. |
| **Autonomous Protocol Pulse** | Phase 1 Mechanic | Step 1 of **Phase 1**, where `1 Black Cube` from the general supply is placed into the **Staging Cup** every round. |
| **Version Switch (Version Advance & Track Spillover)** | Mechanic | When the final space of `v1.0` or `v2.0` is filled in Phase 2, excess Black cubes immediately spill over onto the next Version's **Corruption Track**, but the active Version card remains current for the rest of the round (preserving current **Institutional Override** access through **Phases 3–6**). The official **Version Switch** executes at the start of the next round in Phase 1 Step 1 (forfeiting unspent old overrides and unlocking the new version's override). *(The `v2.0` Market Share comparison for Money is removed as of v0.38.0.)* |
| **Snowball Recycle** | Phase 4 Mechanic | At the end of **Phase 4: Attack Resolution**, all exited **Red, Blue, and Green Attack Cubes** in the **Exit Tray** are placed back into the **Staging Cup** for the next round. |
| **Compile Buffer Overflow (Empty Tray)** | Phase 2 Mechanic | Triggered if `0 cubes` of any color exit the **Cube Tower** during Phase 2. No corruption occurs and no attacks resolve in Phase 4; income is still collected in Phase 3. `1 Red, 1 Blue, and 1 Green Cube` from the supply are added to the **Staging Cup** for next round. |

### 3. Company Terminal Mat & CI/CD Pipeline Zones

| Canonical Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Company Terminal Mat** | Component | A player's personal board containing their 3-zone **CI/CD Pipeline**, 4 **Terminal Hardware & R&D Upgrade Tracks**, **Personal Reserve**, **Institutional Override Track**, and resource tracks/banks. |
| **CI/CD Pipeline** | Board Architecture | The three-zone lifecycle loop on a player's **Company Terminal Mat** through which dice flow: **1. Ready for Release Zone** $\to$ **2. In Production Area** $\to$ **3. Under Development Pipeline** (`Processing Box` $\to$ `Scanned Box`) $\to$ back to **Ready for Release Zone**. Surviving **In Production** dice also return through the **Scanned Box** in **Phase 6**. |
| **Ready for Release Zone** | Mat Zone (Zone 1) | The top staging zone on the **Company Terminal Mat** holding online dice ready to be rolled in **Phase 1**. Starting dice, newly purchased dice, **Breakthrough** dice, graduated **Scanned** dice, and *Magnetic Splice* dice enter this zone. |
| **In Production Area** | Mat Zone (Zone 2) | The central zone on the **Company Terminal Mat** where rolled dice that did not show a **Secret face** are deployed in **Phase 1 Step 4**. Every die here produces its outputs in **Phase 3: Income** (unless returned by a Corruption space in Phase 2). Dice in **In Production** remain deployed through Phases 2–4, then move to the **Scanned Box** in **Phase 6**. |
| **Under Development Pipeline** | Mat Zone (Zone 3) | The bottom offline zone on the **Company Terminal Mat** with two sequential boxes: the **Processing Box** and the **Scanned Box**. Dice in **Under Development** are offline and do not roll in Phase 1. |
| **Processing Box** | Sub-Zone (Zone 3) | The left box in **Under Development** (replaces the Glitched Box, retired in v0.38.0). A die that shows a **Secret face** after Phase 1 rerolls enters here with no other output. When it leaves — **Conveyor Triage** in Phase 6 (oldest first, up to **Triage Bandwidth**) or *Magnetic Splice* — its owner gains the Secret face's yield in **Corporate Secrets**. A die returned to the supply from here, or still here at game end, yields nothing. |
| **Scanned Box** | Sub-Zone (Zone 3) | The right verification box in **Under Development**. In **Phase 6**, all surviving **In Production** dice move to the **Scanned Box**; then up to **Release Bandwidth** dice of the player's choice graduate from **Scanned** to **Ready for Release**, and up to **Triage Bandwidth** dice move from the **Processing Box** to **Scanned** (oldest first). Newly purchased Central Market colored dice (Phase 5) also go here. |
| **Personal Reserve** | Mat Zone | The private holding slot on a player's **Company Terminal Mat** holding that player's 2nd **Purple Proprietary Specialist Die**. Released only by the **Public Relations Track** Level 3 **Breakthrough** (it cannot be purchased). |

### 4. Terminal Hardware Tracks, Capabilities & Resources

| Canonical Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Money (`$` / Liquid Cash)** | Resource | Primary financial currency used to trigger paid Star (`★`) abilities (including *Ad Campaign*), pay for the **Triage Track** Level 3 **Breakthrough**, regain **Market Share** through the **Public Relations Track** (`$3` Cash per `+1`), and purchase **Software Module Dice** into the **Scanned Box**. Second game-end tiebreaker after Market Share. Money is uncapped and never drops below `$0`. |
| **Research Tokens (`🔬`)** | Resource | Scientific/technical currency used to upgrade the 4 **Terminal Hardware Tracks** and to lower **Vulnerability** through the **Pen Test Track** (`2 🔬` per `−1`). Uncapped storage across rounds. |
| **Breakthrough** | Track Reward | A one-time reward printed at **Level 1** and **Level 3** of each **Terminal Hardware Track**, resolved immediately in **Phase 5: Operations** when that level is first reached (before the player's next operation; several levels bought in one turn resolve one at a time in order). Pen Test / Release: L1 gain `1` Gray Legacy die (to **Ready for Release**), L3 gain `1` Corporate Secret. Triage: L1 gain `1` Gray Legacy die (to **Ready for Release**), L3 gain `1` Corporate Secret. Public Relations: L1 gain `$3` Liquid Cash, L3 gain the 2nd Purple die from the **Personal Reserve** (to the **Scanned Box**). A Breakthrough that cannot be resolved is lost. |
| **Corporate Secrets** | Resource / VP | Proprietary client data tokens representing a player's survival health and sole source of Victory Points (`1 Secret = 1 VP`). Start at `5` at every player count (`6` with *Data Scraper* as the `v1.0` card) `[TUNE]`. Gained mainly from **Secret faces** (when the die leaves the **Processing Box**), Blue Face 1, and the **Market Leader Bonus**. Lost to **Blue Cubes**, **Green Cubes** (by **Vulnerability** band) and some **Corruption Track** spaces (never to **Red Cubes** or a lack of Money). Dropping to `<= 0` Corporate Secrets triggers an immediate **Total Data Breach (Patient Zero)**. Ties are broken by the **Hierarchical Tiebreakers** (Market Share first). |
| **Triage Track & Triage Bandwidth** | Hardware Track | Hardware track (Levels `0 → 4`, step costs `1 / 2 / 2 / 3 🔬`) governing **Triage Bandwidth** (`1 / 1 / 2 / 2 / 2`), the maximum number of dice that move from the **Processing Box** to the **Scanned Box** (oldest first) during **Phase 6 Step 4 (Conveyor Triage)**. **Breakthroughs** at Levels 1 (Gray die) and 3 (`+1` Corporate Secret). Level 4 Capstone optionally replaces `1` owned Gray Legacy die with a Central Market colored die of your choice (`$0` free, to **Ready for Release**). |
| **Release Track & Release Bandwidth** | Hardware Track | Hardware track (Levels `0 → 4`, step costs `1 / 2 / 2 / 3 🔬`) governing **Release Bandwidth** (`4 / 4 / 5 / 5 / 6`), the maximum number of dice that can graduate from the **Scanned Box** to the **Ready for Release Zone** during **Phase 6 Step 3 (Scanned Graduation)**. Because surviving **In Production** dice return through the **Scanned Box**, it caps all dice returning to Ready from Scanned each round (purchases and **Breakthrough** dice except PR L3 bypass it). **Breakthroughs** at Levels 1 (Gray die) and 3 (`+1` Corporate Secret). Level 4 Capstone also lets you set `1` rolled Gray Legacy die to any printed face in **Phase 1 Step 3** (after rerolls and *Prodigy*/*Helix* overrides). |
| **Pen Test Track** | Hardware Track | Hardware track (Levels `0 → 4`, step costs `1 / 2 / 2 / 3 🔬`) setting the Pen Test limit (`1 / 1 / 2 / 2 / 3`): the most **Vulnerability** a player may remove in their **Phase 5** Operations Turn, at `2 🔬` per `−1` `[TUNE]` (not below `0`; not a hardware upgrade). **Breakthroughs** at Levels 1 (Gray die) and 3 (`+1` Corporate Secret). Level 3 or higher also adds `+1` to Blue **Secret face** yields. Level 4 Capstone also lowers Vulnerability by `1` for free at the start of each later Operations Turn (outside the limit). *(Replaces the Defense Grid Track, retired in v0.46.0.)* |
| **Public Relations Track** | Hardware Track | Hardware track (Levels `0 → 4`, step costs `1 / 2 / 2 / 3 🔬`) setting how much **Market Share** a player may regain with cash in **Phase 5: Operations**: up to `1` per round at Levels 0–1, `2` at Levels 2–3, `3` at Level 4, at `$3` Cash per `+1` `[TUNE]`; never above the track maximum (`20`). Market Share from die faces and *Brand Protection* / *Ad Campaign* does not count toward this limit. **Breakthroughs** at Levels 1 (`+$3` Cash) and 3 (2nd Purple die from **Personal Reserve** to **Scanned Box**). Level 4 Capstone also grants `+2` **Market Share** immediately. *(Replaces the Cryptographic Patents Track, retired in v0.36.0.)* |
| **Market Share** | Resource / Track | Tracked on the shared **Market Share Track** on the Central SETEC Agent Board (with 1 Market Share Marker per player, numbered `0` to `20`). Starts at `14` (2 players) or `18` (3–4 players) `[TUNE: v0.46.0]`, allowing headroom to grow up to the track maximum of `20`; it cannot drop below `0`. Lowered by `1` per **Red Cube** in Phase 4 and by rivals' *Customer Poaching*; raised in Phase 3 by Red and Yellow Market Share faces, *Brand Protection* and *Ad Campaign*, and in Phase 5 through the **Public Relations Track**. Sets the player's **Market Leader Bonus** place and is the first game-end tiebreaker. A surviving player at `0` triggers a **Market Collapse**. Scores no VP directly. |

### 5. Software Module Dice, Faces & Market

| Canonical Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Software Module Dice** | Component | Custom 6-sided dice (`d6`) representing software and hardware subroutines (`0` blank faces and `0` Glitch faces). Includes **Gray Legacy Dice**, **4 Central Market Colored Dice** (`Red`, `Blue`, `Green`, `Yellow`), and **Purple Proprietary Specialist Dice**. |
| **Gray "Legacy Code" Starter Dice** | Die Type | Starter dice (`2` per player in **Ready for Release** at setup; more from Pen Test / Triage / Release Level 1 **Breakthroughs**). No **Secret face**; three faces carry ⚠ (`+$1 & +1 🔬` ⚠, `+2 🔬` ⚠, Choice `+$2 / +2 🔬` ⚠, Choice `+$1 / +1 🔬`, `+$1`, `+1 🔬`). Cannot be returned or burned as Colored Module Dice. |
| **Colored Software Module Dice (Colored Dice)** | Die Category | Collectively refers to the 5 permanent non-Gray module colors: the **4 Central Market Colors** (**Red [Marketing]**, **Blue [Firewall]**, **Green [Antivirus]**, **Yellow [Commercial AI]**) plus **Purple [Proprietary Specialist]**. Red, Blue, Green and Purple have 4 core faces, Face 5 **Secret face**, and Face 6 `★`; Yellow has 5 core faces and Face 6 `★`. Every Secret face and one high-output core face carry ⚠ (Yellow: Faces 1 and 5); `★` faces never do. Each player starts with 1 Red, Blue or Green die named by their **Client Company**. |
| **Purple Proprietary Specialist Dice** | Die Type | Bespoke operative dice (`2` per player: `1` starting in **Ready for Release**, `1` in **Personal Reserve** until the **Public Relations Track** Level 3 **Breakthrough**). Cannot be purchased (not sold in the Central Market); Face 6 (`Purple ★`) triggers the player's **Signature Protocol**. |
| **Red Dice (Marketing)** | Die Type | The Red Central Market Colored Dice (subtitle **Marketing**). Faces `+1 Market Share / +$1` ⚠, `+$2` ×3, **Secret face** ⚠ (yield `+2` Corporate Secrets `[TUNE]`; also triggers a **Market Leader Bonus** at the end of that round's Phase 6), `Red ★` (defined by the active Red Variant Card: *Brand Protection*, *Ad Campaign* or *Customer Poaching*). |
| **Blue Dice (Firewall)** | Die Type | The Blue Central Market Colored Dice (subtitle **Firewall**). Faces: `+1 Corporate Secret` (collected in Phase 3: Income), `+2 🔬`, `+$2` ⚠, Choice `+1 🔬 / +$1`, **Secret face** ⚠ (yield `+2` Corporate Secrets when leaving the Processing Box, or `+3` if the **Pen Test Track** is at Level 3 or higher), and `Blue ★` (defined by the active Blue Variant Card: *Honeypot Telemetry*, *Deep R&D Vault*, or *Air-Gapped Backup*). |
| ***Brand Protection* (R1)** | Red `★` Variant Card | `$5` `[TUNE]`. Face 6: `+$2 & +1 Market Share` in Phase 3 (a **Dual-Output Face**; not above the track maximum; outside the **Public Relations Track** limit). |
| ***Ad Campaign* (R2)** | Red `★` Variant Card | `$4` `[TUNE]`. Face 6: if the die is still **In Production** in Phase 3, the player may pay `$2` to gain `+2` **Market Share** (not above the track maximum; outside the **Public Relations Track** limit). |
| ***Customer Poaching* (R3)** | Red `★` Variant Card | `$5` `[TUNE]`. Face 6: in Phase 3, after all players' Income (clockwise from the **First Player**), take `1` **Market Share** from the surviving rival with the most Market Share (the player chooses among tied rivals). At maximum, the rival still loses `1` and the player gains nothing. |
| **Software Module Variant Cards (`★` Cards)** | Component | Cards placed face-up in the **Software Market** (1 per Central Market color: **Red, Blue, Green, Yellow**) that define the purchase cost (`$4–$6`) and the Face 6 Special (`★`) effect for that color of die for the match. |
| **Secret face** | Die Face | Face 5 on Red, Blue, Green and Purple dice; it always carries ⚠. It may be kept or rerolled in Phase 1. A die showing it after rerolls moves to the **Processing Box** with no other output; when the die leaves the Processing Box its owner gains Corporate Secrets by color, using the state at that moment `[TUNE]`: Red `+2` (and triggers the **Market Leader Bonus** at the end of Phase 6); Blue `+2`, plus `1` if the **Pen Test Track** is at Level 3+ (max `+3`); Green `+2`, then lower your **Vulnerability** by `2`; Purple `+1`. *Arbitrary Code Execution* cannot set it; *Brute-Force Bypass* can. |
| **Choice Face (`/`)** | Die Face | A die face printed with a slash (`/`) offering two mutually exclusive outputs (e.g., `+1 Market Share / +$1` or `+$1 / +1 🔬`). The player commits to one option during **Phase 1 Step 4**. |
| **Dual-Output Face (`&` / `+`)** | Die Face | A die face that provides two simultaneous outputs (e.g., `+$1 & +1 🔬`, Yellow `+$1 & +1 Market Share`, or *R1 Brand Protection* `+$2 & +1 Market Share`), all collected in Phase 3: Income (if not returned in Phase 2). |

### 6. Phase Mechanics, Damage Resolution & End Game

| Canonical Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Roll & Deploy** | Game Phase | The first phase of each round (Simultaneous): 1. Autonomous Protocol Pulse & Version Switch $\to$ 2. Roll Ready Pool $\to$ 3. Yahtzee Rerolls (then *Prodigy* / *Helix* / Release Level 4 overrides) $\to$ 4. Lock-in & Assign Dice (gain `+1` **Vulnerability** per ⚠ on final faces; **Secret faces** to the **Processing Box**; all other dice **In Production**). |
| **Phase 2: Compiler Drop & Corruption** | Game Phase | The second phase of each round (Simultaneous): Drop the **Staging Cup** into the **Cube Tower**, resolve exited **Black (Corruption) Cubes** on the **Corruption Track** (Version Advance, Spillover, **Singularity**), handle **Compile Buffer Overflow**, then check End Game Triggers. Exited **Attack Cubes** wait in the **Exit Tray** for Phase 4. |
| **Phase 3: Income** | Game Phase | The third phase of each round (Simultaneous): collect `$`, `🔬`, **Corporate Secrets** (Blue Face 1), **Market Share** and `★` outputs from every die still **In Production**; **Meridian Financial Trust's** *Capital Injection* (`+$3` cash) and income-timed **Signature Protocols** (including **The Operator's** *Logistics Bounty*: `+1 🔬` per different color die in your **Scanned Box**) resolve here. *Customer Poaching* resolves after all players' Income. |
| **Phase 4: Attack Resolution** | Game Phase | The fourth phase of each round (Simultaneous; no cube can be blocked): Red (**Market Share**) and Blue (Corporate Secrets) damage for every surviving player, then Green cubes one at a time against the highest **Vulnerability** at `4`+ (band damage, reset to `0`), the **Patient Zero** check, **Target Lock** Signature Attacks, **Snowball Recycle** (in 2P, **SETEC Corp** gains `+1` Market Share if `>= 1` Red cube exited the tray), then the End Game Trigger Check (**Total Wipeout**, **Patient Zero**, then **Market Collapse**). |
| **Phase 5: Operations** | Game Phase | The fifth phase of each round: clockwise **Operations Turns** starting with the **First Player** (upgrade hardware tracks up to 2 times per turn and resolve **Breakthroughs**, regain **Market Share** using Cash [`$3` per `+1`] through the **Public Relations Track**, lower **Vulnerability** through the **Pen Test Track** [`2 🔬` per `−1`], buy up to 1 die into the **Scanned Box**). |
| **Phase 6: CI/CD Pipeline Shift** | Game Phase | The sixth phase of each round (Simultaneous): 1. *Magnetic Splice* $\to$ 2. Production Return (In Production $\to$ **Scanned Box**) $\to$ 3. Scanned Graduation (up to **Release Bandwidth**, dice of the player's choice; newly bought dice graduate here) $\to$ 4. Conveyor Triage (up to **Triage Bandwidth**, **Processing Box** oldest first, banking **Secret face** yields; a Green Secret also lowers **Vulnerability** by `2`) $\to$ 5. **Market Leader Bonus** if a Red Secret face was banked this round, then pass the **First Player Marker**. |
| ***Capital Injection*** | Institutional Override | **Meridian Financial Trust's** once-per-version **Institutional Override**, resolved in **Phase 3: Income**: gain an immediate **`+$3` cash payout** from the bank. *(Replaces Redundant Crew, retired in v0.40.0.)* |
| ***Logistics Bounty*** | Signature Protocol | **The Operator's** **Signature Protocol**, resolved in **Phase 3: Income** whenever Face 6 (`Purple ★`) is still **In Production**: gain **`+1 🔬` Research Token** per different color die currently in your **Scanned Box** (all colors including Gray, Purple, and Yellow). *(Replaces Contingent Staffing, retired in v0.40.0.)* |
| **Total Data Breach (Patient Zero)** | Elimination / End Trigger | Triggered in **Phase 2** (Corruption Track), **Phase 3** (*Deep R&D Vault*) or **Phase 4** (attacks) when a player's **Corporate Secrets** drop to `0` or below (`<= 0`). That player becomes **Patient Zero**, is eliminated from winning, skips the remaining phases of the round, takes no **Market Leader Bonus** place, and triggers the **Final Round**. |
| **Singularity** | End Trigger | Triggered in **Phase 2** when the final (`6th`) space of the `v3.0` **Corruption Track** is covered by a **Black Cube**. After resolving Space 6's printed effect, the game ends **immediately** at the end of Phase 2 (skipping Phases 3–6; dice in the **Processing Box** yield nothing) and proceeds directly to victory scoring, including the game-end **Market Leader Bonus**. |
| **Total Wipeout Ending** | End Game Rule | Occurs if **all** players are **Patient Zero** at the end of **Phase 2** or **Phase 4**; the remaining phases are skipped (no **Market Collapse** check, no Phase 6 **Market Leader Bonus**) and Project: SETEC wins narratively. Human players are ranked **only** by the standard **Hierarchical Tiebreakers** (Market Share > Money > Active Colored Module Dice in Ready for Release > Research Tokens > fewest dice Under Development > shared); breach depth is ignored and no game-end **Market Leader Bonus** is awarded. *(The former Net Wipeout Score is retired as of v0.34.0.)* |
| **Final Round** | End Game Rule | The round in which any player becomes **Patient Zero** or a **Market Collapse** occurs. Surviving players complete every remaining phase of that round (including Phase 6 Conveyor Triage and its **Market Leader Bonus**), then proceed to victory scoring. Regaining Market Share in Phase 5 does not cancel it. |
| **Market Collapse** | End Trigger | Checked at the end of **Phase 4** (after the **Patient Zero** check, and not after a **Total Wipeout**). If any surviving player has `0` **Market Share**, the current round becomes the **Final Round**. No one is eliminated and there is no other penalty; the player at `0` scores normally and is placed for the **Market Leader Bonus** like anyone else (the game-end award is still given). If Patient Zero occurs in the same round, breached players are still eliminated. **Singularity** ends the game in Phase 2 before Market Collapse can be checked. |
| **Market Leader Bonus** | Scoring Rule | Bonus **Corporate Secrets** by **Market Share** place, read at the moment it is awarded: 1st `+3`, 2nd `+1`, other places `0` at every player count `[TUNE]`. Awarded (a) once at the end of **Phase 6** in any round in which at least one Red **Secret face** was banked (by any player, in Phase 3 or Phase 6 Conveyor Triage; several in one round still give one award; also in the **Final Round**), and (b) once at game end on every ending except a **Total Wipeout** (Singularity, Patient Zero and Market Collapse endings included). Only non-**Patient Zero** players (plus **SETEC Corp** in 2-player games, which starts at `0` Market Share, gains `+1` in Phase 4 whenever `>= 1` Red cube exits the tray and can receive the `+2` of a Red **Target Lock** Signature Attack, capped at `20`) take places; tied participants share the better place; a player at `0` Market Share is placed normally. |

### 7. Retired Terms
