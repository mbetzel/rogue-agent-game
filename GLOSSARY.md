## 7. Glossary of Key Terms

> **Terminology Governance:** All changes to `RULES.md`, Print-and-Play assets (`pnp/`), and the Web UI (`web/`) must align strictly with this glossary. Any new term not defined below requires explicit confirmation before being introduced.

### 1. Core Identity & Roles

| Canonical Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Too Many Secrets** | Game Title | The primary title of the board game (codebase/simulation title: **Rogue Agent**). |
| **Project: SETEC (The Rogue Agent)** | Central Threat | The autonomous cracking program that serves as the central shared threat, compiling Attack and Corruption Cubes through the Cube Tower each round across three escalating versions (`v1.0`, `v2.0`, `v3.0`). |
| **White Hat Operative (White Hat Dossier)** | Player Identity & Asymmetric Setup | The specialist operative lead directed by each player (**The Operator**, **The Phreaker**, **The Acoustic**, **The Spook**, **The Prodigy**, **The Cipher**), contracted to defend a **Client Company's** infrastructure. Your **White Hat Dossier Card** defines the **Signature Protocol** triggered when rolling Face 6 (`Purple ★`). |
| **Client Company** | Asymmetric Setup | One of 6 enterprise clients (**Meridian Financial Trust**, **Omnidyne Systems**, **Aegis Defense Relay**, **Department of Telecommunications Oversight [DTO]**, **Helix Biomedical Consortium**, **The Prometheus Foundation**) contracted at setup. Grants a once-per-version **Institutional Override** (DTO *Slush Fund*: once per game) and names the color of the player's starting colored die (Red: Meridian, Prometheus; Blue: DTO, Aegis; Green: Omnidyne, Helix `[TUNE]`). Otherwise all Client Companies start with identical hardware and resources. |
| **Institutional Override** | Ability | A powerful corporate ability printed on a player's **Client Company Card** that may be activated **once per SETEC Version** (`v1.0`, `v2.0`, `v3.0`); DTO *Slush Fund* may be activated only **once per game**. Tracked on the **Institutional Override Track** as **Locked**, **Available**, **Used**, or **Forfeited**. |
| **Signature Protocol** | Ability | The bespoke operative ability printed on a player's **White Hat Dossier Card**, triggered whenever that player rolls Face 6 (`Purple ★`) on a **Purple Proprietary Specialist Die**. |
| **First Player (First Player Marker)** | Turn Order | The token designating the player who takes the first **Operations Turn** in **Phase 6: Operations**. Passes clockwise at the end of Phase 6 each round. |

### 2. Central Threat & Cube Tower Mechanics

| Canonical Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cube Tower (Mainframe Decryption Compiler)** | Component | The physical tower with internal baffles into which cubes from the **Staging Cup** are dropped during **Phase 2: Compiler Drop & Corruption**. Cubes either get stuck on the internal baffles or exit into the **Exit Tray**. |
| **Staging Cup (Relay Queue)** | Component / Zone | The cup holding **Compiler Cubes** queued to be dropped into the **Cube Tower** during the next **Phase 2**. |
| **Exit Tray** | Component / Zone | The tray at the base of the **Cube Tower** where cubes land when they exit the tower during **Phase 2**. |
| **Internal Baffles (Stuck Cubes)** | Mechanic | Ledges inside the **Cube Tower** that trap dropped cubes, holding them in latent suspension until knocked loose in a future round. |
| **Pre-Seed Drop (Prime the Compiler)** | Setup Mechanic | The setup step where `1 Red, 1 Blue, 1 Green, and 1 Black Cube` are dropped into the empty **Cube Tower**; any cubes that exit into the tray are returned to the supply, leaving only stuck cubes inside to prime Round 1. |
| **Compiler Cubes** | Component | The four colors of cubes (**Red**, **Blue**, **Green**, **Black**) processed through the **Cube Tower**. Divided into **Black (Corruption) Cubes** and **Attack Cubes** (`Red`, `Blue`, `Green`). |
| **Attack Cubes** | Threat Category | Collectively refers to **Red**, **Blue**, and **Green** Compiler Cubes, which exit in **Phase 2**, resolve against players in **Phase 4: Attack Resolution**, and then recycle back into the **Staging Cup**. |
| **Red Cubes (Market Manipulation)** | Attack Cube | Attack cube resolved in **Phase 4**. Each unblocked Red cube lowers the player's **Market Share** by `1` (not below `0`). Red cubes never cost Money or **Corporate Secrets**. *(Replaces Ransomware / Financial Wiretaps, retired in v0.36.0.)* |
| **Blue Cubes (Spyware / Data Exfiltration)** | Attack Cube | Attack cube resolved in **Phase 4**. Each unblocked Blue cube steals **`1 Corporate Secret`**. |
| **Green Cubes (Malware / Logic Bombs)** | Attack Cube | Attack cube resolved in **Phase 4**. Each unblocked Green cube turns `1` of the player's shield slots into a **Glitched Shield Slot** (no die moves). A player cannot have more Glitched Shield Slots than their **Defense Grid Capacity**; excess unblocked Green cubes have no effect. |
| **Black Cubes (Corrupted Code / Corruption)** | Threat Cube | Threat cube resolved in **Phase 2: Compiler Drop & Corruption**. Each exited Black cube advances the active **Version Card's Corruption Track** by 1 space and triggers that space's printed effect. Black cubes remain on the track and do not recycle. |
| **SETEC Version Card (`v1.0`, `v2.0`, `v3.0`)** | Component | One of three face-up roadmap cards defining SETEC's starting Staging Cup payload (`v1.0`) and each version's escalating **Corruption Track** (`4` spaces on `v1.0`, `5` spaces on `v2.0`, `6` spaces on `v3.0`). |
| **Corruption Track** | Board Track | The sequence of spaces on the active **SETEC Version Card** (`4 / 5 / 6` spaces). Each space covered by an exited **Black Cube** in Phase 2 immediately triggers its printed hazard (such as **Lose `$X`**, **Lose `X 🔬`**, **Glitch `X` Dice** — which glitches `X` shield slots into **Glitched Shield Slots**, **Return `X` Colored Dice**, **Lose `X` Corporate Secrets**) or injects new cubes into the **Staging Cup**. |
| **Autonomous Protocol Pulse** | Phase 1 Mechanic | Step 1 of **Phase 1**, where `1 Black Cube` from the general supply is placed into the **Staging Cup** every round. |
| **Version Switch (Version Advance & Track Spillover)** | Mechanic | When the final space of `v1.0` or `v2.0` is filled in Phase 2, excess Black cubes immediately spill over onto the next Version's **Corruption Track**, but the active Version card remains current for the rest of the round (preserving current **Institutional Override** access through **Phases 3–6**). The official **Version Switch** executes at the start of the next round in Phase 1 Step 1 (forfeiting unspent old overrides and unlocking the new version's override). *(The `v2.0` Market Share comparison for Money is removed as of v0.38.0.)* |
| **Snowball Recycle** | Phase 4 Mechanic | At the end of **Phase 4: Attack Resolution**, all exited **Red, Blue, and Green Attack Cubes** in the **Exit Tray** are placed back into the **Staging Cup** for the next round. |
| **Compile Buffer Overflow (Empty Tray)** | Phase 2 Mechanic | Triggered if `0 cubes` of any color exit the **Cube Tower** during Phase 2. No corruption occurs and no attacks resolve in Phase 4; income is still collected in Phase 3. `1 Red, 1 Blue, and 1 Green Cube` from the supply are added to the **Staging Cup** for next round. |

### 3. Company Terminal Mat & CI/CD Pipeline Zones

| Canonical Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Company Terminal Mat** | Component | A player's personal board containing their 3-zone **CI/CD Pipeline**, 4 **Terminal Hardware & R&D Upgrade Tracks**, **Personal Reserve**, **Institutional Override Track**, and resource tracks/banks. |
| **CI/CD Pipeline** | Board Architecture | The three-zone lifecycle loop on a player's **Company Terminal Mat** through which dice flow: **1. Ready for Release Zone** $\to$ **2. In Production Area** $\to$ **3. Under Development Pipeline** (`Processing Box` $\to$ `Scanned Box`) $\to$ back to **Ready for Release Zone**. Surviving **In Production** dice also return through the **Scanned Box** in **Phase 5**. |
| **Ready for Release Zone** | Mat Zone (Zone 1) | The top staging zone on the **Company Terminal Mat** holding online dice ready to be rolled in **Phase 1**. Starting dice, newly purchased dice, **Breakthrough** dice, graduated **Scanned** dice, and *Magnetic Splice* dice enter this zone. |
| **In Production Area** | Mat Zone (Zone 2) | The central zone on the **Company Terminal Mat** where rolled dice that did not show a **Secret face** (or a White Contractor `Glitch`) are deployed in **Phase 1 Step 5**. Divided into the **Income Slot** and the **Defense Grid (Shield Slots)**. Dice in **In Production** remain deployed through Phases 2–4, then move to the **Scanned Box** in **Phase 5**. |
| **Income Slot** | Sub-Zone (Zone 2) | The left sub-zone of **In Production** where dice producing Money (`$`), Research (`🔬`), **Market Share**, or non-shield Star (`★`) effects are placed. Collects outputs in **Phase 3: Income** (unless returned by a Corruption space in Phase 2). |
| **Defense Grid (Shield Slots)** | Sub-Zone (Zone 2) | The right sub-zone of **In Production** holding **Generic (`Any`) Shields** (the only shield type). The number of shields placed in Phase 1 Step 5 is limited to **Defense Grid Capacity** minus the player's **Glitched Shield Slots**. |
| **Under Development Pipeline** | Mat Zone (Zone 3) | The bottom offline zone on the **Company Terminal Mat** with two sequential boxes: the **Processing Box** and the **Scanned Box**. Dice in **Under Development** are offline and do not roll in Phase 1. |
| **Processing Box** | Sub-Zone (Zone 3) | The left box in **Under Development** (replaces the Glitched Box, retired in v0.38.0). A die that shows a **Secret face** after Phase 1 rerolls enters here with no other output. When it leaves — **Conveyor Triage** in Phase 5 (oldest first, up to **Triage Bandwidth**), *Magnetic Splice*, *Jumper Diagnostics* or *B3 Air-Gapped Backup* — its owner gains the Secret face's yield in **Corporate Secrets**. A die returned to the supply from here, or still here at game end, yields nothing. |
| **Scanned Box** | Sub-Zone (Zone 3) | The right verification box in **Under Development**. In **Phase 5**, all surviving **In Production** dice move to the **Scanned Box**; then up to **Release Bandwidth** dice of the player's choice graduate from **Scanned** to **Ready for Release**, and up to **Triage Bandwidth** dice move from the **Processing Box** to **Scanned** (oldest first). Dice used to clean a **Glitched Shield Slot** in Phase 1 and *Y3 Proprietary Training* dice also go here. |
| **Personal Reserve** | Mat Zone | The private holding slot on a player's **Company Terminal Mat** holding that player's 2nd **Purple Proprietary Specialist Die**. Released only by the **Public Relations Track** Level 3 **Breakthrough** (it cannot be purchased). |

### 4. Terminal Hardware Tracks, Capabilities & Resources

| Canonical Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Money (`$` / Liquid Cash)** | Resource | Primary financial currency used to hire **White Contractor Dice**, trigger paid Star (`★`) abilities (including *Ad Campaign*), and purchase **Software Module Dice**. Second game-end tiebreaker after Market Share. Money is uncapped and never drops below `$0`. |
| **Research Tokens (`🔬`)** | Resource | Scientific/technical currency used to upgrade the 4 **Terminal Hardware Tracks** and to regain **Market Share** through the **Public Relations Track** (`3 🔬` per `+1`). Uncapped storage across rounds. |
| **Breakthrough** | Track Reward | A one-time reward printed at **Level 1** and **Level 3** of each **Terminal Hardware Track**, resolved immediately in **Phase 6: Operations** when that level is first reached (before the player's next operation; several levels bought in one turn resolve one at a time in order). Defense Grid / Release: L1 gain `1` Gray Legacy die, L3 gain `1` Corporate Secret. Triage: L1 gain `1` Gray Legacy die, L3 optionally pay `$3` to replace `1` Gray Legacy die with a Central Market colored die. Public Relations: L1 gain `$3`, L3 gain the Purple die from the **Personal Reserve**. A Breakthrough that is declined or cannot be resolved is lost. New dice go to the **Ready for Release Zone**. |
| **Corporate Secrets** | Resource / VP | Proprietary client data tokens representing a player's survival health and sole source of Victory Points (`1 Secret = 1 VP`). Start at `3` (2 players) or `4` (3–4 players) `[TUNE]`. Gained mainly from **Secret faces** (when the die leaves the **Processing Box**) and the **Market Leader Bonus**. Lost to unblocked **Blue Cubes** and some **Corruption Track** spaces (never to **Red Cubes** or a lack of Money). Dropping to `<= 0` Corporate Secrets triggers an immediate **Total Data Breach (Patient Zero)**. Ties are broken by the **Hierarchical Tiebreakers** (Market Share first). |
| **Triage Track & Triage Bandwidth** | Hardware Track | Hardware track (Levels `0 → 4`, step costs `1 / 2 / 2 / 3 🔬`) governing **Triage Bandwidth** (`2 / 2 / 3 / 3 / 4`), the maximum number of dice that move from the **Processing Box** to the **Scanned Box** (oldest first) during **Phase 5 Step 4 (Conveyor Triage)**. **Breakthroughs** at Levels 1 and 3. Level 4 also grants `+1 🔬` per die moved in Conveyor Triage beyond the 2nd. |
| **Release Track & Release Bandwidth** | Hardware Track | Hardware track (Levels `0 → 4`, step costs `1 / 2 / 2 / 3 🔬`) governing **Release Bandwidth** (`4 / 4 / 5 / 5 / 6`), the maximum number of dice that can graduate from the **Scanned Box** to the **Ready for Release Zone** during **Phase 5 Step 3 (Scanned Graduation)**. Because surviving **In Production** dice return through the **Scanned Box**, it caps all dice returning to Ready from Scanned each round (purchases and **Breakthrough** dice bypass it). **Breakthroughs** at Levels 1 and 3. Level 4 also grants `1` extra free reroll in **Phase 1 Step 4**. |
| **Defense Grid Track & Defense Grid Capacity (`defense_capacity`)** | Hardware Track | Hardware track (Levels `0 → 4`, step costs `1 / 2 / 2 / 3 🔬`) governing **Defense Grid Capacity** (`2 / 2 / 3 / 3 / 4`), the number of shield slots; the number of dice a player may assign to **Shield Slots** in **Phase 1 Step 5** is capacity minus **Glitched Shield Slots**. **Breakthroughs** at Levels 1 and 3. Level 3 or higher also adds `+1` to Blue **Secret face** yields. Level 4 also pays `+$1` per Attack Cube blocked in **Phase 4** (max `$3`), after damage. A newly gained slot arrives clean. |
| **Public Relations Track** | Hardware Track | Hardware track (Levels `0 → 4`, step costs `1 / 2 / 2 / 3 🔬`) setting how much **Market Share** a player may regain with research in **Phase 6: Operations**: up to `1` per round at Levels 0–1, `2` at Levels 2–3, `3` at Level 4, at `3 🔬` per `+1` `[TUNE]`; never above the track maximum (`20`). Market Share from die faces and *Brand Protection* / *Ad Campaign* does not count toward this limit. **Breakthroughs** at Levels 1 and 3 (L1 gain `$3`, L3 gain the Purple die from the **Personal Reserve**). *(Replaces the Cryptographic Patents Track, retired in v0.36.0.)* |
| **Market Share** | Resource / Track | Tracked on the shared **Market Share Track** on the Central SETEC Agent Board (with 1 Market Share Marker per player, numbered `0` to `20`). Starts at `13` (2 players) or `16` (3–4 players) `[TUNE: v0.38e]`, allowing headroom to grow up to the track maximum of `20`; it cannot drop below `0`. Lowered by `1` per unblocked **Red Cube** in Phase 4 and by rivals' *Customer Poaching*; raised in Phase 3 by Red and Yellow Market Share faces and *Brand Protection*, and in Phase 6 through the **Public Relations Track** and *Ad Campaign*. Sets the player's **Market Leader Bonus** place and is the first game-end tiebreaker. A surviving player at `0` triggers a **Market Collapse**. Scores no VP directly. |

### 5. Software Module Dice, Faces & Market

| Canonical Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Software Module Dice** | Component | Custom 6-sided dice (`d6`) representing software and hardware subroutines. Includes **Gray Legacy Dice**, **White Contractor Dice**, **4 Central Market Colored Dice** (`Red`, `Blue`, `Green`, `Yellow`), and **Purple Proprietary Specialist Dice**. |
| **Gray "Legacy Code" Starter Dice** | Die Type | Starter dice (`2` per player in **Ready for Release** at setup; more from Defense Grid / Triage / Release Level 1 **Breakthroughs**). Reliable baseline with no `Glitch` or **Secret face** (`Block 1 Any` ×2, `+$1 & +1 🔬`, Choice `+$1 / +1 🔬` ×2, `+1 🔬`). Cannot be returned or burned as Colored Module Dice. |
| **White "Contractor" Dice** | Die Type | Temporary freelance dice hired in **Phase 1 Step 2 (Temp Staffing)** using **Progressive Contractor Pricing** (`$1` for 1st, `$2` for 2nd, `$3` for 3rd; max `3` per round). High risk (`2 Glitch` faces, the only Glitch faces in the game). Never enter **Under Development**—they are discarded to the general supply if they roll an unresolved Glitch, are used to clean a **Glitched Shield Slot**, or expire at the end of Phase 4. Contractors still In Production pay their income in Phase 3. |
| **Colored Software Module Dice (Colored Dice)** | Die Category | Collectively refers to the 5 permanent non-Gray, non-White module colors: the **4 Central Market Colors** (**Red [Marketing]**, **Blue [Firewall]**, **Green [Antivirus]**, **Yellow [Commercial AI]**) plus **Purple [Proprietary Specialist]**. Red, Blue, Green and Purple have 4 core faces, Face 5 **Secret face**, and Face 6 `★`; Yellow has 5 core faces and Face 6 `★`. Each player starts with 1 Red, Blue or Green die named by their **Client Company**. |
| **Purple Proprietary Specialist Dice** | Die Type | Bespoke operative dice (`2` per player: `1` starting in **Ready for Release**, `1` in **Personal Reserve** until the **Public Relations Track** Level 3 **Breakthrough**). Cannot be purchased (not sold in the Central Market); Face 6 (`Purple ★`) triggers the player's **Signature Protocol**. |
| **Red Dice (Marketing)** | Die Type | The Red Central Market Colored Dice (subtitle **Marketing**). Faces `+1 Market Share / +$1`, `+$2` ×3, **Secret face** (yield `+2` Corporate Secrets `[TUNE]`; also triggers a **Market Leader Bonus** at the end of that round's Phase 5), `Red ★` (defined by the active Red Variant Card: *Brand Protection*, *Ad Campaign* or *Customer Poaching*). |
| ***Brand Protection* (R1)** | Red `★` Variant Card | `$5` `[TUNE]`. Face 6: `+$2 & +1 Market Share` in Phase 3 (a **Dual-Output Face**; not above maximum; outside the **Public Relations Track** limit). |
| ***Ad Campaign* (R2)** | Red `★` Variant Card | `$4` `[TUNE]`. Face 6: no shield or Income output; if the die is still **In Production** in Phase 3, the player may pay `$2` during their Phase 6 Operations Turn to gain `+1` **Market Share** (not above maximum; outside the **Public Relations Track** limit; lost if unused that turn). |
| ***Customer Poaching* (R3)** | Red `★` Variant Card | `$5` `[TUNE]`. Face 6: no shield or Income output; in Phase 3, after all players' Income (clockwise from the **First Player**), take `1` **Market Share** from the surviving rival with the most Market Share (the player chooses among tied rivals). At maximum, the rival still loses `1` and the player gains nothing. |
| **Software Module Variant Cards (`★` Cards)** | Component | Cards placed face-up in the **Software Market** (1 per Central Market color: **Red, Blue, Green, Yellow**) that define the purchase cost (`$4–$6`) and the Face 6 Special (`★`) effect for that color of die for the match. |
| **Glitch Face (`Glitch`)** | Die Face | An unstable face found only on **White Contractor Dice** (Faces 5 & 6). When rolled in Phase 1, a die showing `Glitch` cannot be rerolled during standard free rerolls. At the end of rerolls, the Contractor is discarded to the supply and produces `0` outputs. |
| **Secret face** | Die Face | Face 5 on Red, Blue, Green and Purple dice. It may be kept or rerolled in Phase 1. A die showing it after rerolls moves to the **Processing Box** with no other output; when the die leaves the Processing Box its owner gains Corporate Secrets by color, using the state at that moment `[TUNE]`: Red `+2`; Blue `+1`, `+1` if the owner blocked a Blue cube this round, `+1` at Defense Grid Level 3+ (max `+3`); Green `3` minus the owner's **Glitched Shield Slots** (min `0`), then cleans `1` Glitched Shield Slot; Purple `+1`. *Arbitrary Code Execution* cannot set it; *Brute-Force Bypass* can. |
| **Choice Face (`/`)** | Die Face | A die face printed with a slash (`/`) offering two mutually exclusive outputs (e.g., `+1 Market Share / +$1` or `+$1 / +1 🔬`). The player commits to one option during **Phase 1 Step 5**. |
| **Dual-Output Face (`&` / `+`)** | Die Face | A die face that provides two simultaneous outputs (e.g., `+$1 & +1 🔬`, Yellow `+$1 & +1 Market Share`, or *R1 Brand Protection* `+$2 & +1 Market Share`), all collected in Phase 3: Income (if not returned in Phase 2). |
| **Generic (`Any`) Shield (`Block 1 Any`)** | Shield Type | The only shield face (found on Gray, White and Purple dice) deployed to the **Defense Grid**. Does not pre-commit to a color in Phase 1; in **Phase 4** the player chooses how all available shields split across the exited Red, Blue and Green cubes (every available shield must block while any unblocked cube remains). A die showing it may instead be placed on a **Glitched Shield Slot** in Phase 1 Step 5 to clean it. |
| **Glitched Shield Slot** | Mat State | A shield slot glitched by an unblocked **Green Cube** (Phase 4) or a **Corruption Track** `Glitch X Dice` space (Phase 2). Each player begins the game with `1` Glitched Shield Slot on their **Company Terminal Mat** (leaving `1` clean slot at baseline Defense Grid Capacity `2`). It cannot hold a shield (capacity = **Defense Grid Capacity** minus Glitched Shield Slots, min `0`), and each one lowers Green **Secret face** yields by `1`. Cleaned in Phase 1 Step 5 by *G1 Self-Healing Kernel* (the die stays In Production) or by placing a `Block 1 Any` die on it (no output; the die goes to the **Scanned Box**, a White Contractor is discarded), or when a Green **Secret face** banks in Phase 5 Step 4. Never more than Defense Grid Capacity; a new Defense Grid slot arrives clean. |

### 6. Phase Mechanics, Damage Resolution & End Game

| Canonical Term | Category | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Roll & Deploy** | Game Phase | The first phase of each round (Simultaneous): 1. Autonomous Protocol Pulse & Version Switch $\to$ 2. Temp Staffing $\to$ 3. Roll Ready Pool $\to$ 4. Yahtzee Rerolls $\to$ 5. Assign Dice (White Contractor `Glitch` discarded; **Secret faces** to the **Processing Box**; clean **Glitched Shield Slots**; shields up to capacity; Income). |
| **Phase 2: Compiler Drop & Corruption** | Game Phase | The second phase of each round (Simultaneous): Drop the **Staging Cup** into the **Cube Tower**, resolve exited **Black (Corruption) Cubes** on the **Corruption Track** (Version Advance, Spillover, **Singularity**), handle **Compile Buffer Overflow**, then check End Game Triggers. Exited **Attack Cubes** wait in the **Exit Tray** for Phase 4. |
| **Phase 3: Income** | Game Phase | The third phase of each round (Simultaneous): collect `$`, `🔬`, **Market Share** and `★` outputs from every die still **In Production** (Income slot and Shield slots, including **White Contractor Dice**); income-timed **Signature Protocols** resolve here. *Customer Poaching* resolves after all players' Income. |
| **Phase 4: Attack Resolution** | Game Phase | The fourth phase of each round (Simultaneous): the player splits all **Generic (`Any`) Shields** across the exited cubes, then Red (**Market Share**) / Blue (Corporate Secrets) / Green (**Glitched Shield Slots**) damage, Defense Grid Level 4 cash, **Snowball Recycle**, Contractor Expiration, then check End Game Triggers (**Total Wipeout**, **Patient Zero**, then **Market Collapse**). |
| **Phase 5: CI/CD Pipeline Shift** | Game Phase | The fifth phase of each round (Simultaneous): 1. *Magnetic Splice* $\to$ 2. Production Return (In Production $\to$ **Scanned Box**) $\to$ 3. Scanned Graduation (up to **Release Bandwidth**, dice of the player's choice) $\to$ 4. Conveyor Triage (up to **Triage Bandwidth**, **Processing Box** oldest first, banking **Secret face** yields; a Green Secret cleans 1 Glitched Shield Slot) $\to$ 5. **Market Leader Bonus** if a Red Secret face was banked this round. |
| **Phase 6: Operations** | Game Phase | The sixth phase of each round: clockwise **Operations Turns** starting with the **First Player** (upgrade hardware tracks and resolve **Breakthroughs**, regain **Market Share** through the **Public Relations Track**, paid `★` actions, buy up to 2 dice into the **Ready for Release Zone**), then pass the **First Player Marker**. |
| **Contractor Sacrifice** | Phase 1 Rule | When a **White Contractor Die** shows an unresolved Glitch at the end of Phase 1 rerolls, it is discarded directly to the general supply instead of entering **Under Development**. |
| **Total Data Breach (Patient Zero)** | Elimination / End Trigger | Triggered in **Phase 2** (Corruption Track) or **Phase 4** (attacks) when a player's **Corporate Secrets** drop to `0` or below (`<= 0`). That player becomes **Patient Zero**, is eliminated from winning, skips the remaining phases of the round, takes no **Market Leader Bonus** place, and triggers the **Final Round**. |
| **Singularity** | End Trigger | Triggered in **Phase 2** when the final (`6th`) space of the `v3.0` **Corruption Track** is covered by a **Black Cube**. After resolving Space 6's printed effect, the game ends **immediately** at the end of Phase 2 (skipping Phases 3–6; dice in the **Processing Box** yield nothing) and proceeds directly to victory scoring, including the game-end **Market Leader Bonus**. |
| **Total Wipeout Ending** | End Game Rule | Occurs if **all** players are **Patient Zero** at the end of **Phase 2** or **Phase 4**; the remaining phases are skipped (no **Market Collapse** check, no Phase 5 **Market Leader Bonus**) and Project: SETEC wins narratively. Human players are ranked **only** by the standard **Hierarchical Tiebreakers** (Market Share > Money > Active Colored Module Dice in Ready for Release > Research Tokens > fewest dice Under Development > shared); breach depth is ignored and no game-end **Market Leader Bonus** is awarded. *(The former Net Wipeout Score is retired as of v0.34.0.)* |
| **Final Round** | End Game Rule | The round in which any player becomes **Patient Zero** or a **Market Collapse** occurs. Surviving players complete every remaining phase of that round (including Phase 5 Conveyor Triage and its **Market Leader Bonus**), then proceed to victory scoring. Regaining Market Share in Phase 6 does not cancel it. |
| **Market Collapse** | End Trigger | Checked at the end of **Phase 4** (after the **Patient Zero** check, and not after a **Total Wipeout**). If any surviving player has `0` **Market Share**, the current round becomes the **Final Round**. No one is eliminated and there is no other penalty; the player at `0` scores normally and is placed for the **Market Leader Bonus** like anyone else (the game-end award is still given). If Patient Zero occurs in the same round, breached players are still eliminated. **Singularity** ends the game in Phase 2 before Market Collapse can be checked. |
| **Market Leader Bonus** | Scoring Rule | Bonus **Corporate Secrets** by **Market Share** place, read at the moment it is awarded: 1st `+3`, 2nd `+1`, other places `0` at every player count `[TUNE]`. Awarded (a) once at the end of **Phase 5** in any round in which at least one Red **Secret face** was banked (by any player, in Phase 3 or Phase 5; several in one round still give one award; also in the **Final Round**), and (b) once at game end on every ending except a **Total Wipeout** (Singularity, Patient Zero and Market Collapse endings included). Only players who are not **Patient Zero** take places; tied players share the better place; a sole survivor takes 1st; a player at `0` Market Share is placed normally. |

### 7. Retired Terms

Do not use these terms in rules, Print-and-Play assets or the Web UI: *Glitched Box* (replaced by **Processing Box**), *Color-Specific Shield* and *Red / Blue / Green Shields* (every shield is a **Generic (`Any`) Shield**), *Two-Stage Defensive Blocking*, *Defensive Shield Sacrifice* (Green no longer glitches dice), and *Hash Match* (all retired in v0.38.0); *Unpaid Ransom Leak*, *Ransomware / Financial Wiretaps* (Red cube theme), *Cryptographic Patents Track*, *Patent Filing*, *FinSec* (Red die subtitle), *Ransom Insurance* (former R1 name), *Threat Bounty* and *Short Squeeze* (former R2 / R3 variants) (all retired in v0.36.0); *Emergency Hotfix* and *Passive Firewall* (Level 2 track perks, retired in v0.35.0), *Personal Reserve Procurement* (Purple purchase, retired in v0.35.0), and *Net Wipeout Score* (retired in v0.34.0).
