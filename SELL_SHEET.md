# TOO MANY SECRETS
### *"They’re breaking the cryptography. Don’t be left holding the bad tape."*

**Players:** 2–4 | **Playtime:** 45–60 Min | **Ages:** 14+ | **Category:** Medium Competitive Survival Euro / Dice-Pool Building  
**Comparable Titles:** *Cubitos* meets *Moon Colony Bloodbath* (with the kinetic tension of *Shogun / Wallenstein*'s Cube Tower)

---

## THE ELEVATOR PITCH
It’s 1992. An autonomous cracking program codenamed **Project: SETEC** has broken loose across ARPANET relays and university mainframes. Each player directs a rival boutique white-hat security consultancy hired to protect corporate secrets. 

Simultaneously roll and upgrade custom dice pools representing 90s hardware and software modules, patch burnt-out magnetic tape relays, and brace for the **Mainframe Compiler (Cube Tower)**. Cash-strapped firms can secretly siphon slush funds from the rogue protocol—injecting corrupted code into the tower and accelerating total cryptographic collapse in hopes of bankrupting their rivals first. When the dust settles, any firm that suffers a **Total Data Breach** becomes **Patient Zero** and is raided by federal marshals; the surviving consultancy with the highest **Portfolio Value** wins!

---

## CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP (8–10 Fast, Simultaneous Rounds)

1. **PHASE 1: BOOT & CONFIGURE *(Simultaneous)***  
   SETEC pulses (+1 Black Corruption Cube to the Staging Cup). Hire temporary night-shift **Phreaker Contractors** (White Dice), roll your **Ready Pool** of custom `d6` software modules, and take up to **2 Yahtzee-style rerolls**. Spend **Tape Splice Tokens (`🔧`)** to debug locked terminals, then commit dice between **Color-Coded Firewalls** and **Revenue / Operations**.
2. **PHASE 2: SETEC COMPILE *(The Cube Tower Drop)***  
   Dump the entire **Staging Cup** into the **Cube Tower**. Stuck cubes create latent dread on the internal baffles; exited cubes resolve immediately:
   * **Black Cubes** advance SETEC from `v1.0` $\rightarrow$ `v2.0` $\rightarrow$ `v3.0`, injecting heavier payloads and hostile system directives toward **Singularity**.
   * **Red (Ransomware), Blue (Spyware), & Green (Logic Bombs)** strike all players simultaneously. Unblocked attacks drain cash, exfiltrate **Corporate Secrets**, or burn out active dice.
   * **The Snowball:** All exited Red, Blue, and Green cubes scoop *right back into the Staging Cup* for next round!
3. **PHASE 3: OPERATIONS & RETAINERS *(Turn Order)***  
   Buy upgraded colored dice from the open **Software Market**, splice worn-out modules back online, or execute a **Backdoor Siphon ("Dirty Income")**—trading 1 Secret and burning a Ready die for an immediate `+$3` cash injection while dropping another Black Cube into the compiler!

## KEY SELLING POINTS FOR PUBLISHERS

* **The "Snowball" Cube Tower Compiler:** Unlike static event decks, SETEC's attack pool physically snowballs every round. Exited attack cubes recycle continuously into the Staging Cup while trapped cubes create explosive, visible suspense players can calculate around.
* **Engine-Building Meets Engine-Wearing:** Custom `d6` dice have **zero blank faces**, ensuring high agency. However, any permanent die that actively blocks an attack wears down into your **Unpatched Zone**—forcing a razor-sharp Euro triage between expanding your engine, defending your clients, and repairing technical debt.
* **The "Patient Zero" Elimination Hook:** You don't have to outrun the Rogue Agent—you just have to outrun the other consultancies. Siphoning dirty income accelerates the clock and weaponizes the Cube Tower against greedy rivals, creating electric table politics with zero direct player-vs-player kingmaking.
* **High Replayability with Zero Mid-Game Card Clutter:** Every game pairs 1 of **6 Asymmetric Consultancy Archetypes** (*The Phreaker, The Spook, The Acoustic, The Cipher, The Prodigy, The Operator*—each with "use-it-or-lose-it" once-per-version powers) with modular `v1.0–v3.0` SETEC cards and **15+ Software Variant (`★`) cards** that redefine custom die faces every match.
* **Standout "Tape-Punk" Aesthetic:** Moves away from overdone neon cyberpunk into tactile 1990s lo-fi cryptography—amber/green monochrome CRTs, reel-to-reel magnetic tape splices, acoustic couplers, and sneaker-net floppy disks.
* **Rigorously Stress-Tested:** Game economy, player-count scaling (2P–4P), and archetype parity have been validated across **50,000+ automated Monte Carlo simulation matches** alongside a full digital browser playable (`web/`).
