01 The Elevator Pitch

It’s 1992. An autonomous cracking program codenamed Project: SETEC has broken loose across ARPANET relays and university mainframes. Each player directs a rival boutique white-hat security consultancy hired to safeguard high-stakes corporate secrets.

Players simultaneously roll custom software module dice, splice burnt-out magnetic tape relays, and brace for the Mainframe Compiler (Cube Tower). Desperate firms can secretly tap SETEC via Backdoor Siphons for slush cash—injecting corrupted code into the tower to accelerate total collapse and bankrupt greedy rivals first. Any firm reduced to 0 Secrets becomes Patient Zero and is raided by federal marshals; the surviving consultancy with the highest Portfolio Value wins!

03 Fast, High-Tension Round Structure

1. Boot & Configure SIMULTANEOUS

SETEC pulses (+1Black Cube to Staging Cup). Optionally hire temp White Contractor Dice ($1/$2/$3), roll your Ready Pool, and take up to 2 Yahtzee rerolls. Spend Tape Splices (🔧) to debug locked faces, assign Shields, and collect $ & 🔧 outputs.

2. SETEC Compile TOWER DROP

Drop the Staging Cup into the Cube Tower! Exited Black cubes advance SETEC (v1.0→v3.0) toward Singularity. Unblocked Red drains $ (or leaks Secrets), Blue steals Secrets, and Green wears/destroys dice. Blocking dice move to Unpatched; exited R/B/G cubes recycle!

3. Operations Turn CLOCKWISE

Purchase up to 2 Colored Module Dice ($4–$5) from the open Market directly into Ready, repair worn Unpatched dice (1🔧 or $2 each), or trigger a Backdoor Siphon—trading Secrets and technical debt for emergency capital while accelerating SETEC!

05 Why Players & Publishers Love It

Kinetic “Snowball” Cube Tower: Exited attack cubes scoop right back into the Staging Cup every round while stuck cubes accumulate on internal baffles—turning visible probability math into electric table suspense.
Engine-Building Meets Engine-Wearing: Custom d6 modules feature zero blank faces for maximum tactical agency, but actively blocking attacks wears permanent dice offline—forcing razor-sharp Euro repair triage.
“Patient Zero” Semi-Survival Hook: You don’t need to outrun SETEC—just outlast rival firms. Weaponizing Backdoor Siphons accelerates system corruption to crack greedy engines without direct PvP kingmaking.
Modular Setup, Zero Mid-Game Card Clutter: 6 Asymmetric Consultancies (with use-it-or-lose-it once-per-version abilities), v1.0–v3.0 SETEC directives, and 15 Variant (★) cards redefine custom dice every game.
Standout 1992 “Tape-Punk” Aesthetic: Breaks away from neon cyberpunk clichés into tactile analog-digital tradecraft—amber CRTs, magnetic tape splices, acoustic couplers, and sneaker-net cryptography (Sneakers meets WarGames).
